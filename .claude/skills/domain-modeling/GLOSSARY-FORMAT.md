# GLOSSARY.md format

```md
# <Project name>

<One sentence on what this domain is.>

## Language

**Snapshot**:
<One or two sentences on what it is.>
_Avoid_: dump, export
```

## Rules
- **One term per concept.** Pick the best word and list the rest under `_Avoid_`.
- **Keep definitions tight.** One or two sentences that say what it is, not what it does.
- **Project terms only.** General programming concepts stay out, even when the project uses them heavily.
- **Group under subheadings** once natural clusters appear; until then a flat list is fine.
